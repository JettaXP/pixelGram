pixelGram for Android

A Telegram client in Material 3 Expressive, now on your phone. Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and TDLib inside, Google's design outside. Free and open source.

downloadDownload APK arm64 sendTelegram channel

Android 8.0 or newer. For older or 32-bit phones there is a universal APK.

construction

This is an alpha

pixelGram for Android is brand new. A lot already works, but expect bugs and rough edges, and updates almost every day. Found a bug? Tell us in the channel or in GitHub Issues.

What's already there

Much of it comes from pixelGram for desktop, some of it is made just for the phone.

palette

Material 3 Expressive

Material You colors from your wallpaper, Material shapes, spring animations, segmented lists and menus.

music_note

Music player

A full screen player with word by word lyrics, a queue and cover art.

cast

Chromecast

Send music to your TV or speaker right from the player.

auto_stories

Stories

Watch and post stories, react to them.

Formatting

Bold, italic, monospace, links, quotes and blurred spoilers right from the selection menu.

Quick reactions, avatars of who reacted, animated premium emoji.

How to install

pixelGram isn't in app stores yet, so you install the APK yourself.

  1. Download the APKFits almost every modern phone (arm64). Download
  2. Allow the installAndroid will ask to allow installs from your browser or file manager. Allow it once.
  3. Sign in to TelegramWith your phone number or a QR code from another device. Updates install over the old version and keep your data.
