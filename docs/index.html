Telegram, in Material 3 Expressive

pixelGram is a desktop Telegram client for Windows and Linux. Kotlin, Compose Multiplatform and TDLib underneath, Google's Material Design on top.

Everything you use every day

Chats, groups, channels and bots, with the details that make Telegram feel like Telegram.

palette

Material You colors

The whole color scheme is generated from one accent with Google's Material color utilities. On Linux it can follow your wallpaper through matugen.

interests

Expressive shapes

Avatars, QR codes and the lock screen use the official MaterialShapes.

mic

Voice and music

Record voice messages with a live waveform. Play Opus, MP3, M4A and FLAC in the mini player.

add_reaction

Stickers and emoji

Animated TGS and WebM video stickers, GIFs, custom emoji, Noto or Noto 3D emoji.

forward

Forwarding done right

A forward bar above the message field. Hide the sender or the caption, or change the recipient.

folder_special

Folders

In a navigation rail or as tabs, with icons, Unread and Mentions folders.

forum

Comments and bots

Channel comments, inline bots, keyboards and command menus.

account_circle

Profiles

Profile banners, profile music, username QR codes and clickable @usernames in bios.

lock

Privacy and passcode

Privacy rules, active sessions, blocking, and a local passcode with a lock screen built from Material shapes.

system_update

Updates itself

New versions come straight from GitHub Releases.

notifications

Notifications

System notifications over D-Bus or notify-send, or pixelGram's own pop-ups.

battery_saver

Battery saver

Turn down animations and background work on a laptop.

Username QR codes

Every profile, group and channel has a QR code made of Material shapes, with eyes that slowly spin. The sign-in QR code looks the same. Type any username and scan it with your phone.
